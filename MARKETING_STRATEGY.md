# Zero-Cost Content Strategy: The "Soul Ripple" Effect

## Mission
To turn every visitor into an ambassador of light, creating a viral loop of spiritual sharing.

## Phase 1: The First 30 Days (Foundation)

### Week 1: "The Call to Peace" (Visual Impact)
*   **Platform**: TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
*   **Content Type**: 15-second spiritual aesthetic videos.
*   **Format**: 
    1.  Beautiful nature/church background (use "Royal & Divine" colors).
    2.  Text overlay: "Stop scrolling. Take 1 deep breath. God is with you."
    3.  Sound: Ethereal choir or silence.
    4.  Caption: "Your daily moment of peace. #SoulGuidance #Peace #Jesus"
    5.  **Goal**: 5,000 Impressions.

### Week 2: "The Prayer Chain" (Viral Loop)
*   **Platform**: WhatsApp / Telegram
*   **Content Type**: Text-based Prayer Card (Image).
*   **Format**: 
    1.  High-quality image of the "Saint Michael Prayer" card from your site.
    2.  Message: "A powerful protection prayer for difficult times. Send this to 3 people you want God to protect today."
    3.  **Goal**: 50 Shares.

### Week 3: "The Answer" (Problem/Solution)
*   **Platform**: Facebook Groups (Christian/Prayer groups)
*   **Content Type**: Helpful "Guide" posts.
*   **Topic**: "3 Ancient Prayers for When You Feel Anxious."
*   **Action**: Link back to the specific "Prayer" section on your website.
*   **Goal**: 100 Website Clicks.

### Week 4: "Testimony Tuesday" (Social Proof)
*   **Platform**: All
*   **Content Type**: Anonymous Story.
*   **Format**: "Someone visited our site feeling lost last week. Today, they told us they found hope again. You are not alone."
*   **Goal**: Deep Engagement (Comments/Messages).

## Key Metric (The "One Metric That Matters")
**"Shares per Visitor"**: If 1 in 10 visitors shares the site, you win.

## Tools (Zero Cost)
*   **Canva (Free)**: For creating the social images.
*   **CapCut (Free)**: For editing the 15s videos.
*   **ChatGPT (Free)**: For writing captions.
