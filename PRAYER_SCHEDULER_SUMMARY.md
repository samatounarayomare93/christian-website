# Prayer Scheduler Implementation Summary

## What I Built

I've created a comprehensive **Prayer Scheduler** system that makes it incredibly easy for people to know what to pray and when. This intelligent system provides personalized prayer recommendations, automated reminders, and progress tracking to help maintain a consistent spiritual life.

## Key Features Implemented

### 🕐 **Smart Prayer Times**
- **Morning Prayer (6:00 AM)**: Start the day with God
- **Noon Prayer (12:00 PM)**: Midday reflection
- **Divine Mercy (3:00 PM)**: Most important prayer time with special emphasis
- **Evening Prayer (6:00 PM)**: Thanksgiving and reflection
- **Night Prayer (9:00 PM)**: Peaceful surrender before sleep

### 🔔 **Intelligent Notifications**
- Browser notifications at prayer times
- Visual alerts with prayer content

### 📊 **Progress Tracking**

### 🎯 **Personalized Recommendations**
- Time-based suggestions (morning, afternoon, evening)
- Day-specific recommendations (Sunday Rosary, etc.)
- Progress-based guidance
- Spiritual growth insights

### ⚙️ **Full Customization**
- Adjustable prayer times to fit personal schedules
- Notification preferences (sound, browser, reminders)
- Language support (English and Arabic)
- Personal prayer history storage
